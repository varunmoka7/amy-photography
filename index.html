About

Hi, I’m Amy.

I’m a portrait photographer from Vancouver, Canada, now calling Berlin home. My love for photography began in a high-school darkroom, developing film and printing black-and-white photographs by hand — watching an image slowly come to life felt like magic, and I knew then that photography would always be a part of me.

That curiosity became my career: eight years as a studio photographer taught me how to connect with people, put them at ease, and capture genuine expressions in just a few moments. Today I photograph individuals, couples, and families — preserving the quiet moments, genuine laughter, and little connections that become more meaningful with time.
